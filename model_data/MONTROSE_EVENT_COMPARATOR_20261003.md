## Frozen matching design

`montrose_event_pair_protocol.json` was committed separately at
`fe095ba0c7d70d2618da99133029bcd7569bdac1` **before any pair scoring**.
No event/control pair has been scored in this work. All eight original matching
dimensions are frozen: exact physical subsegment/mask, circular season ≤30 days,
actual paired frame clock ≤20 minutes with clock uncertainty ≤60 seconds,
three actual frames spaced 10±2 seconds, same tide source/method/datum/phase
within 30 minutes, same QC-accepted transport source/depth/reference within
30° and 0.1 m/s when both available, and comparable private/public/social-or-human
observation effort. Both unavailable transport channels are allowed with an
explicit limitation; one available/one unavailable is unresolved. Unknown tide
blocks pairing. Predictions never masquerade as observed tide. Nominal burst
times need independently reconstructed actual timing bounds before admission.

These are prespecified conservative **exploratory screens**, not demonstrated
ecological equivalence. Do not optimize or relax them to manufacture the first
pair. Independent pixel review, adequate detectability, source timestamps/QC,
event footprint and direct Jubilee-class biology remain admission requirements.
Event onset/ending and unsampled intervals remain UNKNOWN. Episode/date blocking
prevents serial bursts from becoming independent cases; one pair cannot establish
predictive performance or calibrate probabilities.
