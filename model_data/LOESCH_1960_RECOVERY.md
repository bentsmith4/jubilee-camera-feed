## Result

No 1946–1956 event row is admitted. The [publisher record](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2307/1930218) confirms *Ecology* 41(2), pp. 292–298, DOI 10.2307/1930218. Its PDF link redirected to the abstract/citation page in this retrieval; neither the seven article pages nor an event table was obtained. [JSTOR's indexed excerpt](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1930218) exposes a fragment that says 1950 had ten Jubilees, but the accessible landing page does not expose the whole article or dates. A search of indexed Alabama Department of Archives and History records yielded the already canonical 1959 and 1972 photographs, not a new exact 1946–1956 date.

## Admission and deduplication

The canonical history has 15 confirmed event records and zero dated 1946–1956 events at this review. The 1959 Point Clear and 1971–1972 rows fall outside Loesch's 1946–1956 window; overlapping source mentions do not create additional events. A future recovery must transcribe the primary page/table or contemporaneous record with page or archive locator; retain the stated date precision and actual shoreline scope; compare event date, place, and source lineage against canonical records; and review each unique event before promotion. Year/month counts, undated place mentions, and publication dates without event chronology remain aggregates or leads.
