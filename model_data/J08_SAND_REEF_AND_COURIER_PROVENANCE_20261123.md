## Sand Reef paired-height evidence

### What the primary and archived sources support

- Patterson, Boettcher, and Carmichael (2014), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0104440`, report 2010 Sand Reef daily dissolved-oxygen summaries at 0.1 m and 1.0 m above the sediment surface. Average daily oxygen was significantly higher at 1.0 m than at 0.1 m.
- The paper's independent full-study ranges were 0.52–12.72 mg/L at 1.0 m and 0.07–9.91 mg/L at 0.1 m. The two minima are not a concurrent gradient.
- GRIIDC dataset `Y1.x027.000:0004` describes 15-minute YSI measurements summarized to daily mean, standard error, maximum, and minimum for Sand Reef top and bottom sheets. The public dataset description does not establish times of the daily extrema or release a subdaily series.
- NOAA/NCEI accession `0125573` v1.1 preserves the original workbook. The exact date-only Sand Reef rows for 2010-08-12 are:

| Height above bed | Daily mean DO | Daily minimum DO | Daily maximum DO |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.1 m | 4.441 mg/L | 1.18 mg/L | 5.70 mg/L |
| 1.0 m | 4.921 mg/L | 3.11 mg/L | 5.83 mg/L |

The workbook sheets do not provide the extreme time, sample size, timezone, or a released subdaily observation file. Denton Reef is summarized over two-week periods and is not a date-matched substitute for the Sand Reef daily sheets. NOAA's CSV and PDF are derived products.

Preserve one episode with two source groups; exact beach, pocket identity, and dawn overlap remain unresolved. Hauge's 2013 post is separate.
- **2018-08-26, Point Clear locality:** Burgard's first-person Atlas Obscura account is date-anchored by the source JPEG metadata. Calls just after 05:00 are notifications, not sighting clocks or onset. Two reported pockets remain one episode; intermittent hotel checks are not a negative. Literal camera clocks near 06:08 and 06:43 with IPTC `+0000`, without GPS or an EXIF offset, do not establish civil time.

These are source-review candidates, not timed scoring admissions in this receipt.
